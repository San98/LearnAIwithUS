Frequently Asked Questions

What happens after I register and pay?

Once you register via the Google Form and complete the payment, you will receive a confirmation email within 24 hours. This email will contain the Zoom link for the course, a detailed schedule, and links to any required materials. Please check your spam folder if you don't see it.

Do I need any special software?

No special paid software is required! All you need is a computer with a stable internet connection, a modern web browser (like Chrome or Firefox), and a Zoom account. We will be using free tiers of services like ChatGPT, Zapier, and n8n, and will guide you through the setup process.

Will the sessions be recorded?

Yes, all sessions will be recorded. If you miss a live session or want to review the material, you will have access to the recordings for 30 days after the course concludes.

Is there a certificate upon completion?

Yes, upon successful completion of the course, you will receive a digital certificate of completion from LearnAIwithUS, which you can share on your LinkedIn profile and resume.

GenAI Foundations

199 SAR

This is the perfect starting point. We'll cover the essential concepts behind Generative AI, introduce you to the most popular tools, and get you started with basic automation so you can see the power of AI in action immediately.

What You'll Learn:

  • The history and core principles of Generative AI.
  • Effective prompting techniques for tools like ChatGPT.
  • How to automate simple tasks by connecting apps with Zapier or n8n.
  • Understanding the landscape of modern AI tools and their uses.

Who is this for?

Absolute beginners, students, professionals, or anyone curious about AI with no prior technical experience.

Ready to build? In this course, you'll move from theory to practice. We focus on creating complex, multi-step automated workflows that solve real-world problems, integrating various AI tools and services together.

Building AI Agents

399 SAR

This is where AI becomes autonomous. You'll learn the architecture and principles behind AI agents that can reason, plan, and execute tasks independently. We will guide you through building and deploying your own functional AI agent.

What You'll Learn:

  • Core concepts of AI agent loops (e.g., ReAct framework).
  • Giving agents access to tools (like web search, calculators).
  • Memory and state management for long-running tasks.
  • A capstone project where you design and build a custom AI agent to solve a specific problem.

Who is this for?

Developers, advanced users, and business leaders who want to understand and build the next generation of AI applications.
